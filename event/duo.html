What's new in Brightened 1.1

A free update for everyone who has Brightened, and the same one-time purchase for everyone who does not yet.

Now on Apple Watch

A watch app with your seven-day average, its stage, and today's minutes on the same gradient, readable in always-on. Complications put the number on your watch face, and stay legible on faces tinted to a single color.

Room to spread out

On iPad and iPhone Duo, the gradient stands as a full-height panel beside your day and your week, and History lays its weeks out side by side.

Widgets that keep up

Home screen widgets now refresh in the background as your watch records daylight, so they stay current even when you have not opened the app in days.

One goal, everywhere

Change your daily goal on your phone and your watch follows.
